Common leaf problems

What to look for

🟤

Early blight

Brown spots with concentric rings, usually on older leaves first.

⚪

Powdery mildew

White, dusty coating on the leaf surface in warm, humid conditions.

🟡

Leaf rust

Orange or yellow pustules on the underside of leaves.

⚫

Leaf spot

Dark, water-soaked spots that may merge and kill the leaf.

General care tips

Healthy habits prevent most problems

💧 Water at the base of the plant and avoid wetting the leaves.
🍃 Remove and dispose of infected leaves promptly.
🌬️ Give plants space for good airflow.
🔄 Rotate crops each season where possible.
